# Mail of Azeroth

Send reagents to your character with the matching profession, and trade goods nobody needs to your bank alt. For WoW Forever, by DinX. Works on its own, and fits Casters, Roads, Scrum and Damage of Azeroth.

## How it works

1. Log in each of your characters once: Mail of Azeroth remembers their professions, realm and faction.
2. Mark your bank alt: `/moa bank` on that character (or in the settings).
3. At a mailbox a panel shows, per character, which reagents can go there. Click **Attach**, then **Send**. Every mail is two clicks of yours; nothing is ever sent by itself.

Rules: a reagent goes to the character with the highest skill in a profession that uses it, unless you have that profession yourself. Right-click an item in the panel to keep it from now on. Only your characters on the same realm and faction are offered.

## Commands

- `/moa`: settings
- `/moa bank`: this character is (not) your bank alt
- `/moa alts`: your characters and their professions

# Mail of Azeroth (Nederlands)

Stuur reagents naar je karakter met het passende beroep, en trade goods die niemand nodig heeft naar je bank-alt. Log elk karakter één keer in, duid je bank-alt aan met `/moa bank`, en klik bij de mailbox op **Bijvoegen** en daarna **Versturen**. Niets wordt vanzelf verstuurd.
